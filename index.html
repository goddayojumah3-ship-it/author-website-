About the Author

Godday Ferdinard Ojumah

Godday Ferdinard Ojumah is a Nigerian author and digital writer. His writing is shaped by an interest in the difficult questions people carry through seasons of poverty, rejection, exhaustion, faith and uncertainty.

His books are written for ordinary people facing real pressure. Rather than pretending that every struggle has an easy answer, his work seeks to give readers language for what they are going through and a reason to keep looking for hope.

Explore the books, read the descriptions and choose the stories and messages that speak to your season.

The Writing Mission

Reach the reader who needs the message.

These books are built around a simple belief: difficult seasons deserve honest words. Some readers need encouragement. Some need faith. Some need someone to describe the struggle they cannot explain. The goal is to create books that meet people where they are.
