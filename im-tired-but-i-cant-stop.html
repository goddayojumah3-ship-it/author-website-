Book by Godday Ferdinard Ojumah

I'm Tired, But I Can't Stop

The Silent Battles of Those Who Refuse to Give Up

This book is a compassionate, reflective companion for anyone who has whispered “I'm tired” yet keeps moving forward. It explores the invisible struggles of survival, unspoken exhaustion, quiet courage, and the quiet determination to keep going when life feels overwhelming.

About This Book

Through honest questions, personal reflection, letters, and encouragement, this book reminds readers that being tired does not mean the story is over. It invites the reader to pause, reflect, find courage in small steps, and keep moving forward even when the road feels too heavy.

Who This Book Is For

It is for anyone carrying invisible burdens, facing emotional exhaustion, struggling through difficult seasons, or simply needing a reminder that they do not have to give up.
